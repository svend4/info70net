Safety & privacy

Sicherheit & Datenschutz – einfach erklärt

These four prototypes work in the browser without an account, advertising, analytics, live AI or a project backend. This page explains exactly what each tool keeps and what happens when you follow an external link.

Page reviewed 12 July 2026

What you can expect

Four promises for this prototype

01

No account or child profile

The tools do not ask for a birth date, email address or sign-in.

02

No tracking or advertising

No analytics, advertising pixels, third-party scripts, fonts or embeds are loaded.

03

Small, visible storage

Automatic progress stays in the current tab. Longer storage needs a clear action.

04

Human help for serious moments

Help Bridge offers planning steps but cannot contact an adult, platform or emergency service.

Your control

Remove Small Steps data for this browser and tab

This removes persistent Small Steps keys for the current website origin and session data belonging to this tab. It does not touch unrelated XAMPP applications.

It cannot erase browser history, hosting logs or data held by an external site. Other already-open tabs keep their own session data and in-memory values until they are cleared or closed.

Important limits

Before relying on a tool

External sources can change

Safe Search is a time-bounded prototype starting list, not a guarantee or full destination audit. Each card shows its review date and domain. Opening it sends normal network information, such as an IP address, to that external site.

Help Bridge is not an emergency service

If danger is immediate, move toward a safer place and call the local emergency number (112 in Germany and across the EU), or ask a nearby adult to call. The site cannot call or report for you.

This is a development prototype

The repository contains policy controls and an applicability review, but it does not claim legal or accessibility conformance. Human safeguarding, legal and accessibility reviews remain release gates.
