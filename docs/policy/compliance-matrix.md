# Compliance applicability matrix

**Review date:** 2026-07-12

**Scope:** `svend4/info70net`, including Safe Search, Media Time, Help Bridge, and AI Literacy Lab

**Status:** Engineering and product guidance; not legal advice or a certification of compliance

## How to read this matrix

The current repository is a dependency-free static prototype. It has no account
system, backend, advertising, user-generated content (UGC), remote analytics, or
live AI integration. Browser state stays on the device unless a user follows an
external Safe Search link. Help Bridge is a planning aid, not an emergency,
counselling, or reporting service.

The production column is intentionally conditional. A fresh applicability review
is required before adding accounts, server-side data, UGC, messaging, payments,
advertising, age assurance, AI, or deployment by a public-sector body.

## Review triggers

Review this matrix before any of the following is merged or procured:

- accounts, authentication, cloud sync, analytics, or remote logging;
- messaging, profiles, UGC, uploads, moderation, or reporting;
- AI models, AI APIs, recommendation systems, or automated triage;
- payments, subscriptions, sponsorship, advertising, or affiliate links;
- age assurance, identity checks, biometrics, or parental monitoring;
- native-app distribution, public-sector deployment, or a material audience change;
- a relevant legal, regulatory, standards, or official-guidance update.

The review must record the product facts, legal scope assumptions, decision owner,
evidence consulted, unresolved questions, and controls accepted. Where the answer
depends on facts or law beyond the team's competence, obtain qualified advice; do
not represent this matrix as a legal guarantee.
